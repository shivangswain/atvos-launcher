atvOS Launcher is a home screen for Google TV and Android TV. It puts your favourite apps in a glass dock, fills the screen with what each app recommends, and keeps the rest of your apps in a tidy grid below. Its design is inspired by the tvOS Home Screen.

Top Shelf
Move to an app in the dock and its top shelf fills the screen: a full-screen picture with the title, details and a Play button, or a row of Continue Watching and Recommended programs. Move through the pages of an app without opening it.

Your Layout
• Put your favourite apps in the dock and reorder the rest.
• Make folders and hide the apps that you don't use.
• Hold Select on an app for its menu: edit the home screen, move it to a folder, choose its top shelf, or delete it.

Control Center
Select the clock for power, network, Do Not Disturb, Bluetooth and a sleep timer, with shortcuts to inputs, accessibility, the TV's settings and search.

Made for the Big Screen
• Glass that blurs and tints what is behind it, drawn smoothly on everyday TV hardware.
• Light or dark appearance, and a 12- or 24-hour clock.
• Your Google profile picture next to the clock (optional).
• TalkBack reads every tile, button and menu option aloud.

Private by Design
atvOS Launcher has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Your layout and settings stay on your TV. It uses the network only for top shelf artwork and your profile photo, over HTTPS.

Set Up
On first launch, atvOS shows each step with a picture of the TV's Settings screen. On many Google TV and Android TV devices, the built-in home screen keeps the Home button. To make the Home button open atvOS, setup turns on Developer options and USB debugging, and then turns off the built-in home screen through the TV's own debugging connection. It sends only fixed commands: to turn the built-in home screen off or back on, and to turn Remote Buttons on or off. You can turn USB debugging off again when setup is done. Or skip the developer settings: turn on atvOS Launcher Remote Buttons in Accessibility, and atvOS opens when you press Home. Restore Default Launcher in atvOS Settings brings the built-in home screen back at any time.

Remote Buttons: hold Home to open Control Center, and press the Settings button for the TV's settings. It is an accessibility service that gets the remote's buttons, acts only on Home and Settings, and never reads the screen or sends anything.
